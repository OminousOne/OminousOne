server authoritative · 60 tick/s · you are the left paddle

Your inputs travel to the server and the world travels back, each taking half the latency. With client prediction off, the paddle you see is the server's delayed version of you: move the mouse and wait for it. Turn prediction on and your paddle obeys you instantly while the server catches up. One honest detail: the ball always bounces off the server's idea of your paddle, not the one you see. Making that gap unnoticeable is what netcode is.
